Empowered by Equality and Equity
Competent Human Resources and Inclusive Culture!

At Limak Cement, we view our employees as the cornerstone of our strategic success. We have redesigned our human resources processes with a focus on sustainable growth, supporting them through talent management and an inclusive corporate culture. In line with our goals, we aim to further strengthen our commitment to equality and equity.

Empowered by Equality and Justice
Shaping the Workforce of the Future!

Limak Cement Global Academy (LCGA) provides training focused on a green future and sustainability, supporting the professional development of our employees. We prepare our human resources for the workforce of the future by adopting the ILO's Green Jobs approach. The Academy strengthens the foundations of the Triple Transformation Project by enhancing environmental awareness and technical competencies. 

In Every Step Towards the Future,
Digital Transformation is by Our Side!

Our digital transformation strategy reshapes our processes with modern technologies to enhance efficiency and reduce our carbon footprint. Through our Center of Excellence (LCoEDX), we aim to bring Türkiye's first Green Data Center to life by 2027. We align with the demands of the digital era by optimizing our processes through data analytics and monitoring systems. 

We Improve Efficiency
Through Innovation and a Sustainable Future

Our R&D Center develops projects centered around sustainability and the circular economy, in line with our 2030 carbon reduction and 2050 net-zero emission targets. We mitigate environmental impacts by enhancing operational efficiency through innovative technologies and Industry 4.0 principles. We support sustainable development through our projects that drive innovation in the industry.
